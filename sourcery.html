It sounds moderately hard, and most of the difficulty is UI.

The math is simple.

  • For a set of checked curves, the region is the part inside every checked curve and outside every other one.
  • A Venn diagram has exactly one such region for every subset; the empty set's region is the outside.
  • Turning the drawing by τ/n carries that region onto the region whose curves are each one place over. So the rotate button is a cyclic shift of the checkboxes plus an animation.
